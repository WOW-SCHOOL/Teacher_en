WOW SCHOOL — automatic submission without Google Apps Script

1. Open: https://web3forms.com/
2. Enter the email: wow.school.english@gmail.com
3. Confirm it and copy the Access Key.
4. Open the config.js file.
5. Find the line:
   window.WOW_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY = "";
6. Paste the key between the quotation marks.
7. Upload the files to GitHub Pages, replacing the previous version.

After that, the teacher clicks “Submit application,” and the full application is automatically sent to wow.school.english@gmail.com.
